# Commerce Market

## Mode

Persuade

## Purpose

Help shoppers discover real products, compare price and availability, understand variants and fulfilment, and reach the existing cart and checkout confidently on desktop and mobile.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A merchant's buying book turns the storefront into a working product list rather than a promotional poster. Product media, price, availability, and order actions arrive as one legible merchandising system. It refuses both the generic soft-card grid and the image-heavy lookbook that hides the work of shopping.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm stock paper, merchant-green ink, vermilion registration marks, tenant-controlled action colour, ruled rows, square image wells, tabular prices, and compact catalogue labels form the system. Photography remains untinted. Shadows and decorative gradients are removed from transaction surfaces.

**STORY:** The visitor sees the tenant's actual offer and shop action, moves directly into real product or collection rows, compares factual product information, inspects variants and stock, then carries the same visual language through cart and checkout without changing any commerce rules.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The shared header is followed by a compact catalogue register naming the tenant and current route. The homepage keeps the merchant's real hero media and copy, but reduces its height so the first real product row begins in or immediately after the opening viewport. The primary shop action is unmistakable and no fabricated promotion, discount, scarcity, or delivery promise is introduced.

**FORM:** Merchant buying book, the selected grounded direction for concept seed `72a94dfc`. Its signature interaction is a horizontal tenant-supplied catalogue index whose active destination is registered by text, route semantics, and a vermilion mark.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- Keep every public route functional through the shared controllers and typed view models.
- Preserve real product, variant, inventory, pricing, tax, discount, shipping, gift-card, cart, checkout, payment, and tracking behavior.
- Preserve tenant appearance tokens, localization, module gates, SEO, preview safety, and Classic fallback.
- Never fabricate discounts, urgency, stock, delivery speed, reviews, best-seller status, product claims, media, or promotions.
- Use only tenant-provided navigation and content in the additional catalogue register.
