# Informational Editorial

## Mode

Read

## Purpose

Help visitors understand an organisation or subject, move confidently through nested information, read long-form pages and articles, and find the next relevant topic or contact path without turning every section into a sales pitch.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A civic reference folio makes a large body of tenant information feel findable and dependable. It refuses the ornamental magazine homepage and the generic card dashboard; hierarchy is carried by indexed bands, running heads, reading measure, and deliberate topic sequences.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool white reading paper sits inside an ink-blue frame, with the merchant primary colour controlling active tabs and focus. Saffron markers identify the reader's position without carrying meaning alone. Square-edged panels, one-pixel rules, numbered folio marks, tabular metadata, and merchant typography form the component language; shadows are absent.

**STORY:** The visitor encounters the organisation's real introduction, sees the principal topics or pages, moves into recent or selected articles and structured information, and leaves through a related page, newsletter, or contact action only when the tenant supplies one.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact shared header is followed by a full-width ink-blue information masthead. The tenant name and current route occupy a fixed running-head band; the actual page title and opening copy sit in a calm left reading column, while a right index panel lists available real destinations. On narrow screens, the index becomes a horizontal tab strip below the title. No invented topics or claims fill missing content.

**FORM:** Civic reference folio, candidate 4 of the grounded publishing directions; concept seed `7b6a4244`. Its signature move is a route index marker that shifts with the current top-level destination while standard links and reading order remain unchanged.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- Keep all public routes functional through shared controllers and typed view models.
- Preserve tenant appearance tokens, real data, localization, module gates, transactions, SEO, preview safety, and Classic fallback.
- Never fabricate authors, publication dates, claims, statistics, topics, media, or institutional proof.
- Treat missing media and sparse content as honest structural states.
