# Service Studio

## Mode

Persuade

## Purpose

Help a visitor understand a service provider, assess fit and credibility from merchant-supplied information, inspect services and process, and reach a contact or enquiry action without friction.

## Direction contract

**World:** A contemporary exhibition guide: quiet authority, clear wayfinding, editorial pacing, factual captions, and generous material space. It is not a literal museum imitation and never turns content into decorative scenery.

**First viewport:** The merchant's proposition reads as the exhibition title, the primary enquiry is immediately visible, and real merchant media acts as evidence. A restrained index/wayfinding layer makes Services and Contact findable within seconds.

**Visitor path:** Proposition → service catalogue → process → merchant-supplied evidence and statistics → supporting editorial material → enquiry/contact.

**Signature interaction:** A slim route marker follows section and navigation structure. Motion is limited to one measured reveal and stops under reduced-motion preferences.

**Cross-surface reach:** The same guide grammar supports service details, about/story, contact, blog, commerce, checkout, operational routes, and custom pages without changing their business logic.

**Material and type:** Warm mineral paper surfaces, deep ink, hairline rules, and the merchant's primary color as a controlled wayfinding accent. Merchant heading and body fonts remain authoritative; hierarchy comes from editorial scale, weight, measure, and spacing.

**States:** Loading, empty, missing-media, disabled-module, long-content, localization, error, focus, and reduced-motion states stay legible and honest. Missing media becomes structured space, never invented imagery.

**Risk:** The guide language can feel institutional when content is sparse. Counter it with direct contact actions, clear service labels, merchant color, and flexible empty states rather than decorative filler.

## Invariants

- Never invent testimonials, clients, metrics, credentials, claims, urgency, or services.
- Keep every public route functional.
- Reuse shared tenant, API, cart, checkout, form, SEO, localization, and module controllers.
- Disable navigation and submissions in Website Editor preview.
- Preserve Classic and safe fallback behavior.
